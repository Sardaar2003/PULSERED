# PulseRed Connect - Chrome Extension

Connect your Reddit account to PulseRed in **1 click** with zero passwords, zero DevTools, and zero cloud datacenter IP blocks.

## How to Install (Takes 10 Seconds)

1. Open **Google Chrome**, **Microsoft Edge**, or **Brave**.
2. Go to `chrome://extensions/` (or `edge://extensions/`).
3. Toggle on **"Developer mode"** in the top right corner.
4. Click **"Load unpacked"** in the top left.
5. Select this folder: `e:\Akhil_Mohanty_Reddit_Test\extension`
6. Done! The **PulseRed Connect** icon will appear in your browser toolbar.

## How to Connect Your Account

1. Open [reddit.com](https://www.reddit.com) in your browser (logged in as `u/Wild-Western-6399`).
2. Click the **PulseRed Connect** extension icon in your browser toolbar.
3. Click **"⚡ Connect to PulseRed"**.
4. Done! Your Reddit account is instantly linked to PulseRed for automated outreach and live DMs.
